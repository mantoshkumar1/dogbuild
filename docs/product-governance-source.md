## Accepted constraints

- DogBuild is the one active product; unrelated ideas remain parked or rejected.
- Founder-only operation continues before sustained revenue.
- Direct operating cost must remain low; unnecessary hosted infrastructure is
  not justified.
- Paid model usage must not be silently absorbed by the founder; future product
  automation must use explicit customer-controlled credentials and billing.
- Human authority is reserved for activation, abandonment, spending, hiring,
  publication, pushing, and material scope changes.
- External demand, willingness to pay, pricing, and distribution remain
  unvalidated. Founder dogfooding proves utility only for the founder.
- The six-month first-payment window begins at launch, not private development.
- Early commercial stopping requires evidence against the central hypothesis.
